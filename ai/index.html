Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and Global Registrar Guidelines

1. Information We Collect

AetherAI Technologies Inc. operates on a strict zero-retention foundation model architecture. When you visit our website, we only process basic technical log data (IP address, browser user-agent, referrers) strictly for DDoS mitigation, load balancing, and network diagnostics.

2. Zero-Retention Guarantee for AI Workloads

Customer data, prompts, and tokens processed via AetherAI foundation endpoints are processed ephemerally in volatile memory. No customer prompts are ever saved, stored, logged to disk, or utilized for retraining any generative artificial intelligence models without explicit written bilateral agreement.

3. Cookies and Telemetry

We use essential security cookies strictly required for CSRF protection, session management, and traffic optimization. We do not sell personal data to data brokers or third-party behavioral advertising networks.

4. Your Rights under GDPR and CCPA

You have the full right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any technical telemetry we maintain. You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@aether-ai.cloud.

5. Security Standards

All communication channels are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with 256-bit encryption. System components conform to ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 Type II controls.
